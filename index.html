The 3 Big Ideas (If You Remember Nothing Else, Read This)

Every single test question and Game Jam requirement comes back to these three rules.

1. Physics = Forces, Not Coordinates

You don’t tell a RigidBody exact X/Y coordinates every frame. You push it with a force or impulse (gravity, a click, a bounce) and let Godot’s engine calculate the slide.

2. Collision = A Two-Way Conversation

Two objects only react if their collision layers/masks (or groups) are set to notice each other. A Signal is the text message one node sends your script when they touch.

3. Loops = Replacing Repetition With a Rule

Never copy-paste the same code 200 times. A for loop tells Godot: "Repeat this block until you hit the target count." Used for spawning stars, enemies, and timers.

Quick Vocab Decoder (No-Fluff Edition)

Godot Term Plain-English Meaning Where You Use It
RigidBody (2D/3D) A node moved by physics forces (gravity, pushes) instead of manual coordinates. Physics player, crates, and your downhill skier.
StaticBody3D A solid object that never moves when hit—walls, floors, and obstacles. The ski slope and trees in Collision.
Area (2D/3D) An invisible trigger zone that detects clicks or overlaps without acting like a solid wall. Detecting mouse clicks on each balloon.
Signal An alert a node fires off when an event happens so your script can react. input_event (balloon click) & body_entered (hitting a tree).
For Loop Code that repeats automatically a set number of times. Spawning 200 random stars in 10 lines of code.

Stuck on Balloon Popper? Use These Guided Hints

Click the problem you’re hitting below. Read the clue and try fixing it yourself before asking for help!

❓ "I click on the balloon, and literally nothing happens." Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Check 3 things in order:

  1. Node Hierarchy: Is your root Balloon node an Area3D (not just a MeshInstance3D)?
  2. Hitbox: Does Area3D have a CollisionShape3D child with a SphereShape3D sized around the balloon?
  3. Signal Connection: Look at the Node → Signals tab on Area3D. Do you see a green arrow next to input_event pointing to _on_input_event?
❓ "The balloon grows just by moving my mouse over it, or grows twice per click!" Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Why this happens: input_event fires for every mouse movement, button press, and button release.

Where to look: At the very top of _on_input_event, you need three "guard clauses" that return early if: (1) it's not a mouse button, (2) it's not the left button, or (3) event.pressed is false (meaning you let go of the button).

❓ "My game crashes with 'Invalid call. Nonexistent function increase_score' when a balloon pops." Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Why this happens: In balloon.gd, $".." looks at the balloon's parent node in the Scene tree.

How to trace it: Check your Scene dock. Is every balloon a direct child of Main, and does Main have balloon_manager.gd attached? Also check spelling: increase_score must match letter-for-letter in both scripts.

❓ "My score label says 'Score: 0' and never changes, or throws a String + int error." Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Check 2 things:

  • Node Name: Did you rename your Label node to ScoreText (capital S, capital T, no spaces) so $ScoreText can find it?
  • Type Conversion: Godot cannot add a number directly to text. Wrap your score variable inside str(...) when setting score_text.text.

✅ Week 8 Pacing Checklist

🆘

Stuck on 2D Physics? Use These Guided Hints

Physics bugs usually come down to Inspector settings or vector math. Check these clues:

❓ "As soon as I hit Play, my player and crates fall straight off the bottom of the screen!" Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Why this happens: By default, every RigidBody2D has gravity turned on (top-down view vs. side-scroller view).

Where to look: Select your Player and Crate nodes, look in the Inspector under RigidBody2D, and set Gravity Scale to 0.0.

❓ "My player flies in the WRONG direction when I click, or barely moves at all." Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Check 2 things:

  1. Direction Order: Think of a.direction_to(b) as drawing an arrow from A to B. You want the arrow pointing from global_position (the player) TO get_global_mouse_position()—not backwards!
  2. Force vs. Mass: If you increased your player's Mass to 5.0, a small hit_force won't budge it. Try increasing hit_force (around 50) or testing values live in Debug mode.
❓ "My player slides forever like an air-hockey puck and never slows down." Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Where to look: In the Inspector for your RigidBody2D, expand the Linear section. Increase Damp (try 1.0 for the player and 3.0 for crates). Damping acts like friction/air resistance.

❓ "My pixel-art character and crate look blurry!" Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Quick Fix: Go to Project → Project Settings → Rendering → Textures and change Default Texture Filter from Linear to Nearest.

✅ Week 9 Pacing Checklist

🆘

Stuck on Collision or Loops? Use These Guided Hints

This is the buggiest week of the unit—use these targeted clues before raising your hand:

❓ [Collision] "My skier hits a tree, bounces off it, and the level NEVER restarts!" Show Hint + Hide Hint −

90% of the time, it's one of these 3 things:

  1. Contact Monitor (Most Common!): By default, a RigidBody3D ignores collision signals! Select Player, open Solver in the Inspector, turn Contact Monitor ON, and set Max Contacts Reported to 1 (or higher).
  2. Group Spelling: Open tree.tscn, check Node → Groups, and make sure the group name matches your code's is_in_group("...") exact capitalization ("Tree" vs "tree").
  3. Signal Connection: Verify body_entered is connected to _on_body_entered on the Player node.
❓ [Collision] "I get a red Debugger error: 'Removing a CollisionObject node during a physics callback is not allowed'" Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Why this happens: Godot is still mid-calculation on the crash when you tell it to delete the level.

How to fix it: Instead of calling reload_current_scene() directly, chain .call_deferred() onto the end of it so Godot waits until the physics frame finishes.

❓ [Collision] "My skier spins out of control like a top when I press Left or Right." Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Where to look: Select the Player's CollisionShape3D and check its Transform → Position. Make sure X and Z are both 0.0 so the force pushes through the center of the player, not off-center.

❓ [Loops] "Only ONE star shows up on screen, even though spawn_count is 200!" Show Hint + Hide Hint −

Check your indentation! In GDScript, indentation tells Godot what belongs inside the loop. If star.position.x = ... is not tabbed inward under the for i in spawn_count: line, all 200 stars stack right on top of each other at (0, 0)!
